# For Advanced learners, the focus should shift from everyday actions to precision, nuance, academic English, professional communication, and idiomatic usage.
Advanced Level

To keep a clear progression:

Beginner
  Basic actions (eat, go, run, sleep)
  Daily life verbs

Intermediate
  Communication
  Work
  Travel
  Technology
  Phrasal verbs
  Abstract thinking verbs

Advanced
  Academic verbs (justify, demonstrate, infer)
  Business verbs (delegate, collaborate, implement)
  Formal verbs (obtain, maintain, pursue)
  Nuanced verbs (hesitate, underestimate, anticipate)
  Idiomatic and advanced phrasal verbs

This structure scales very well because you can add lessons, quizzes, flashcards, and sentence-building exercises inside each category without reorganizing the app later.



For Advanced learners, the focus should shift from everyday actions to precision, nuance, academic English, professional communication, and idiomatic usage.

Advanced Verb Categories

1. Academic Verbs 🎓
  Used in essays, reports, and formal discussions.

  Examples:

  analyze
  evaluate
  demonstrate
  justify
  infer
  illustrate
  formulate
  synthesize

2. Business & Leadership 💼
  Used in professional environments.

  Examples:

  implement
  delegate
  coordinate
  negotiate
  facilitate
  supervise
  streamline
  optimize

3. Critical Thinking 🧠
  Used to express reasoning and complex ideas.

  Examples:

  deduce
  speculate
  anticipate
  assess
  interpret
  reconsider
  distinguish
  conclude

4. Communication & Persuasion 🗣️
  Used in debates, presentations, and negotiations.

  Examples:

  persuade
  emphasize
  clarify
  advocate
  articulate
  convey
  elaborate
  refute

5. Emotions & Psychology ❤️
  More nuanced than basic verbs like "love" or "hate".

  Examples:

  appreciate
  admire
  resent
  sympathize
  regret
  reassure
  frustrate
  overwhelm

6. Social & Professional Interaction 🤝
  Used in relationships and workplace situations.

  Examples:

  collaborate
  engage
  mentor
  influence
  acknowledge
  encourage
  consult
  mediate

7. Law, Government & Society ⚖️
  Useful for news, formal English, and exams.

  Examples:

  regulate
  enforce
  prohibit
  authorize
  legislate
  prosecute
  advocate
  govern

8. Science & Technology 🔬
  Common in technical and scientific contexts.

  Examples:

  innovate
  engineer
  automate
  simulate
  calculate
  develop
  integrate
  configure

9. Advanced Phrasal Verbs 🔄
  High-frequency phrasal verbs used by native speakers.

  Examples:

  come across
  bring about
  look down on
  put up with
  carry out
  phase out
  narrow down
  brush up on


10. Formal Alternatives ✨
Teach learners how to replace simple verbs with more sophisticated ones.
| Basic | Advanced                |
| ----- | ----------------------- |
| get   | obtain, acquire         |
| show  | demonstrate, illustrate |
| help  | assist, facilitate      |
| start | initiate, commence      |
| end   | conclude, terminate     |
| keep  | maintain, preserve      |
| use   | utilize, employ         |
